Zuo Yuezi · Sitting the Month

Postpartum is meant to shape you, not break you

Traditional cultures honor a 30–40 day rest after birth: warmth, nourishing food, deep rest, and being cared for by others. What happens in this window can echo through a mother's health for years.

  • Supports hormone balance and emotional well-being
  • Calms the "wired but tired" nervous system
  • Replenishes blood and qi to ease fatigue
  • Supports uterine recovery and eases aches and tension
  • Strengthens digestion with warm, nourishing foods
  • Supports breast milk production and circulation
  • Helps protect against chronic pain and depletion
  • Gives you time to process birth and become a mother
