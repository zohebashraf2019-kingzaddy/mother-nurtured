Why mothers

A mission born from my own postpartum

I have a deep passion for supporting mothers, especially during the sacred transformation in early postpartum when she and baby are still merged as one energetic unit.

I experienced the power of this stage first-hand with the birth of my son in 2025. I constantly observed how we mirrored each other in every way. Each time there were hiccups, indigestion, skin flare ups, disturbed sleep, etc., it almost always traced back to me: the stories I was telling myself, the suppressed urge to ask for help when I needed it, the worry I swallowed, the physical pains that cried for attention.

But I also experienced the power of relief for us both when I decided to ask, receive, and give grace to myself in those times of vulnerability. To my surprise, I came out stronger, happier, and more alive than I ever felt before pregnancy, with my son thriving too.

That is my mission and prayer for all mothers. Postpartum is meant to shape us and uplift us into our most powerful selves, not break us down into a pile of crumbs.
